A heritage we love, practised with respect.

Falconry is one of the oldest partnerships between people and birds, and a living heritage across the Gulf. Falconry Ethiopia was founded by people who share that love, and who wanted falconers to have a place to fly their birds on open land, legally and without compromise.

We host a small number of guests at a time. That keeps the experience private, unhurried and personal: your falcons, your pace, your traditions, with our team handling everything else on the ground.

The falcon is not a weapon. It is a partner, and the hunt is the conversation between you.

The hunting grounds of Aysha.

Our concession lies in the Aysha zone of the Somali Region, a wide semi-arid lowland of open plains, acacia scrub and dry watercourses. The land is large enough that guests hunt in privacy, without crowding and without other parties in sight.

Flat to gently rolling ground gives falcons long, clear flights. Scattered acacia and scrub hold small game, and the wide skies suit both the stoop and the long chase. Camps sit on higher, shaded ground away from the hunting areas. [Confirm terrain details]

Questions, answered plainly.

Is falcon hunting in Ethiopia legal?

Yes, when it is done under a valid license. Falconry Ethiopia holds an official falcon hunting license from the Ethiopian Wildlife Conservation Authority (EWCA), and all hunts take place inside our licensed concession, within the permitted species and seasons. Hunting outside a licensed area is not allowed.

Which permits do I need?

You need a valid passport and an Ethiopian visa. Hunting permits for the concession are arranged by us. If you bring falcons, you also need CITES permits and veterinary health certificates. We will give you a checklist for your country.

How do I bring my falcons into Ethiopia?

Falcons are listed under CITES, so you need export and import permits from the relevant authorities, plus a veterinary health certificate, and clearance from the Ethiopian veterinary authority on arrival. Allow several weeks. We help you prepare the paperwork and meet you at the airport.

Is it safe?

Safety is part of the plan. Guests travel with our team from airport to camp, the grounds are guided at all times, and we brief you on terrain, heat and local conditions before you begin. We also monitor current travel advice for the region. [Add your safety and insurance details]

When is the best season?

The cooler months are best for you and for your falcons. [Confirm season dates against the permit calendar] Contact us and we will recommend dates that suit your birds and your schedule.

What should I bring?

Your falcons and your own falconry equipment, passport and visa, health certificates and permits, light layered clothing, a hat, sun protection and sturdy boots. We provide camp equipment, meals and transport.

Which animals can be hunted?

Only small game permitted under our license, such as pigeons, other small birds and rabbits. We do not hunt protected species, and we do not allow any hunting beyond the permitted limits.

Is my information kept private?

Yes. We use your details only to reply to your inquiry and to plan your trip. We do not sell or share them, and we do not publish guest names or photos without your permission.

All hunting takes place only within the licensed concession, for permitted species, in permitted seasons and within permitted limits.
